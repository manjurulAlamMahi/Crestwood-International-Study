About Crestwood International
Crestwood International represents a dynamic institution of higher education. Its philosophy is dedicated to setting the standard for quality teaching that leads to overseas degrees and meets the demands of a fast-growing market economy. It recognises the transformation of Bangladesh into a knowledge-based society that needs a highly skilled and knowledgeable workforce.

Crestwood International began its journey in January 2010. It is uniquely positioned in the Bangladesh education field as a learning-facilitation brand. Its programmes are delivered in collaboration with Westbridge University and Kingsford Awards, UK, in an environment that encourages students to achieve excellence in their pursuit of overseas qualifications and degrees immediately after school or high-school graduation.

Our Vision
To be the most trusted gateway to UK higher education in Bangladesh.

Our Mission
To deliver affordable, high-quality UK programmes that produce confident and employable graduates.
