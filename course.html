Overview:

This course gives you a unique combination of computing, business and technical knowledge, so you can work as a System Analyst, Programmer, Network Administrator or IT Consultant. The Higher National Diploma in Computing is designed to meet the growing need for high-quality IT professionals. This Level 5 pathway also lets students progress to a BSc in Information Technology at Crestwood International, or transfer their credits to universities around the world.

About the Programme:

The course develops students' academic and practical skills in programming, database management, information security, web design and development, networking, software engineering, data analytics, business intelligence, project management and a computing research project. On successful completion, students receive a UK certificate from Kingsford Awards (demo), which they can use to enter the job market or progress to the final year of a degree.

Why choose this programme:

The HND programme uses up-to-date modules that prepare students for real-world challenges and a competitive job market. Students learn C/C++, Java, C#, PHP, .NET and JavaScript, along with algorithms and programming paradigms. The database and web modules cover the core functions of databases and website design. The networking modules cover network infrastructure, server administration and security.

How you'll learn:

The course is about applying information technology to the needs of individuals and organisations. It gives you a thorough grounding in the principles of IT, including best practice in web technologies such as HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript.

You will be taught through a combination of lectures, tutorials, seminars, workshops and practical sessions. Your progress is assessed through assignments, exams, presentations, lab work and individual projects, with a strong focus on practical work.
